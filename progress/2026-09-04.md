## What We Have Obtained

### 1. A frozen reference model and recipe

- Model: pinned public RosettaCommons RFD3NA `RFD3` implementation.
- Parameter count: **6,849,538**.
- One shared architecture represents Protein Binder, Antibody CDR-H3, and RNA
  Binding Design.
- The model has not been replaced by a custom Cartesian Transformer.
- The retained Stage 2 recipe uses design-only sequence supervision normalized per
  active design token, joint coordinate/sequence loss, AdamW at `5e-4`, EMA `0.999`,
  official EDM coordinate diffusion, and the official-family 50-step sampler.

The detailed public-recipe comparison is recorded in
[`docs/RFD3NA_RECIPE_AUDIT.md`](../docs/RFD3NA_RECIPE_AUDIT.md).

### 2. A deterministic learnability evaluator

The original evaluator used deposited PDB coordinate frames that did not reproduce
the centered and rigidly augmented frame seen by RFD3NA during training. This caused
a false negative: on the same Binder 12K checkpoint, recovery changed from **10.14%**
under the old frame to **44.86%** after the deterministic frame correction.

The corrected versioned evaluator now has:

- frozen example identities, seeds, diffusion timesteps, coordinate noise, and
  sequence corruption;
- deterministic `model.eval()` execution;
- continuous loss, raw sequence CE, sequence recovery, and coordinate-denoising
  metrics;
- separate semantics for cheap learnability evaluation and true generation
  evaluation;
- Perfect > Perturbed > Broken sanity ordering for all three tasks;
- exactly `0.0` maximum metric difference over three repeated evaluations of the
  same 18K checkpoint.

### 3. Stage 2 proves 32-example learnability

The final frozen overfit-32 results are:

| Task | Untrained recovery | Selected recovery | Correct context | Sequence-shuffled | Spatially detached |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Binder | 1.29% | **44.81%** | 44.81% | 40.47% | 25.79% |
| H3 | 1.79% | **55.69%** | 55.69% | 43.27% | 36.60% |
| RNA | 5.86% | **70.13%** | 70.13% | 69.34% | 67.39% |

The selected checkpoints saw 12K Binder, 6K H3, and 24K RNA samples. These unequal
budgets diagnose task learnability only; they are not the production budget.

All selected checkpoints generated **8/8 distinct sequences** with finite
coordinates. Mean dominant-token fractions were 31.04% for Binder, 33.87% for H3,
and 37.28% for RNA, so the earlier homopolymer collapse is resolved on this panel.

Binder and H3 sequence predictions respond strongly to shuffled or spatially
detached context. RNA is a one-to-many sequence task, so its decisive context signal
is geometric: at the official training distribution's median noise, design-coordinate
RMSD worsens from **1.168 Å** with correct context to **1.212 Å** after sequence
shuffle and **1.278 Å** after spatial detachment.

For Binder, validation recovery increased from the matched 0K audit's 1.54% to

20.15% after spatial detachment; detachment changes 44.41% of design-token

but Stage 3 still requires the final H3/RNA audits and all PASS controls.

while H3 coordinate RMSD worsens from 0.437 Å to 0.460 Å and 0.676 Å.

correct-context advantage. At median training noise, detachment moves its predicted

## Root Causes Resolved So Far

1. **Evaluator coordinate-frame mismatch:** fixed with the versioned deterministic v2
   evaluation frame.
2. **Design-token gradient dilution:** design sequence loss is normalized over active
   design tokens rather than the much larger fixed context.
3. **RNA underexposure in the 32-example diagnostic:** continuing the unchanged joint
   recipe removed generation collapse and produced strong memorization.

The following Stage 2 branches did not solve the shared problem and will not be
repeated in Stage 3: sequence-only training, near-clean sequence-only RNA, full-valid
sequence supervision, sequence-loss weight 1.0, online-only weights, isolated EMA or
optimizer changes, AF3 schedule substitution, and disabling coordinate augmentation.

## What Is Not Yet Proven

- The recipe has not yet passed the 128-example gate.
- Formal single-task baselines on the complete frozen data pools are not yet complete.
- Model-capacity calibration has not started.
- Unified-mixture and multi-task-transfer conclusions have not been established.
- The final samples-seen budget has not been selected.
- Multi-seed true-generation results and a NanoDesign final reference baseline do not
  yet exist.
- NanoDesign is therefore **not yet declared ready for AutoResearch**.
