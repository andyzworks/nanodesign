# NanoDesign Progress — 2026-09-07

## Current stage

Stage 4 — Formal Single-Task Baselines: **RUNNING**.

The authoritative gate state is maintained in
[`docs/PROGRESS.md`](../docs/PROGRESS.md). Stage 3 is complete and passed; the
current work is determining the minimum single-task budget that yields stable,
non-collapsed Binder, H3, and RNA baselines.

## Results already committed

- Stage 3 passed for Binder, H3, and RNA.
- RNA 24K reached 41.18% recovery on the frozen 128-example training panel.
- RNA 24K generation was finite and distinct for 8/8 examples, with
  60.90%/78.79% mean/maximum dominant-token fractions.
- RNA spatial-context detachment at median training noise worsened coordinate
  RMSD from 1.4187 to 1.4911 Angstrom.
- Formal single-task 6K frozen-panel recovery was 15.63% Binder, 39.21% H3, and
  30.28% RNA.
- H3 6K generation was non-collapsed. Binder 6K remained highly concentrated,
  and RNA 6K retained homopolymer collapse, so Stage 4 did not pass at 6K.
